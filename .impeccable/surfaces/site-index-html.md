# Surface brief — site/index.html (landing, all languages)

Scope: single-page site, Persuade mode. Visitor: woman deciding on colour/cut/bridal in Valencia, mostly on phone from Instagram/Google; must believe the work is the best blonde/balayage in Valencia and that Irina protects hair health; action: compose a booking request (service, day, time) that opens WhatsApp.
Proof: real portfolio photos with her shade names, before/after pairs, 5.0★ / 197 Google reviews, milestones (MFW Valencia 2025, Premios Mediterrani, Hair Fest BCN, Oneshot 2026), backstage video.
Constraints: ES/EN/RU, prices in config ("price after consultation"), static, Vercel.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an issue of a fashion monthly called IRINA — cover, contents, editor's letter, shade index, letters, colophon — and its booking is the magazine's bind-in reply card. Refuses the category default: full-bleed salon hero + "Book now" + three service cards on beige.
OWN-WORLD: Espresso cover stock (#1d1411) and gloss-white inner pages, honey as the only chroma; Bodoni Moda Didone masthead at poster scale (Prata for Cyrillic), Jost for cover lines, folios and credits; folio numbers, issue line, photo credits, cover lines, pull quotes, perforation edge on the reply card.
STORY: Cover sells the result and the score; editor's letter states the rule (hair health before sale); shade index proves range by name; before/after proves skill; letters prove trust; reply card books in her language.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: espresso cover, masthead IRINA DINGA spanning full width top, vertical portrait photo right-of-centre full height, cover lines stacked left (lead line = balayage/blonde claim, 5,0★ 197 reseñas, Nº15 años), a tip-on reply card bottom-left with service chips that opens booking. Mobile: photo full-bleed under masthead, cover lines over photo bottom, tip-on card pinned below.
FORM: Fashion magazine issue — position 4 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key b4c6a158 (degraded roll, no challengers).
SIGNATURE: Shade index — hover/tap a shade name in the contents-style list, the plate swaps; plus drag before/after; plus reply-card booking that "tears off" into WhatsApp.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
