# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step and no runtime dependencies; deployed to Vercel (owner's brief).

## Users

- Women in Valencia (and visiting) considering a colour change — balayage, blonde, bronde, grey integration — or a cut, styling or bridal look. They arrive from Instagram, TikTok, Google Maps or word of mouth, usually on a phone, and want to judge the result quality and book.
- Multilingual: Spanish speakers, Russian/Ukrainian speakers, Romanian speakers (Moldova), English-speaking expats and tourists. Some clients fly in (Switzerland, Berlin, Ibiza).
- Secondary: the owner (Irina) showing the site to clients and partners.

## Product Purpose

Personal website and booking front for Irina Dinga, hairstylist in Valencia. Success = more qualified booking requests in WhatsApp, with the client already knowing service, preferred date and time.

## Positioning

Solo author studio: one stylist, 15 years of experience, colour specialist (balayage and blonde). Clients repeatedly confirm: hair health over sale (she declines to lighten hair that is not ready), long consultation, colour matched to skin, eyes and brows, calm perfectionism, a beautiful salon. Owner confirmed: solo studio, no team on the site.

## Operating Context

- Booking today happens via WhatsApp +34 623 920 999 and Instagram DM. No calendar backend yet; the site composes a ready WhatsApp message (service / date / time / name / language). A real calendar must be pluggable later without redesign.
- Address: C/ Puerto Rico, 51, L'Eixample (Ruzafa), 46004 València. Near Estació del Nord, Parque Central.
- Hours: Mon–Fri 10:00–20:00, Sat 9:00–14:00, Sun closed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages ES / EN / RU. Default: browser language, fallback ES; switcher always visible.
- No prices yet: show "price after consultation"; prices and durations live in a config to fill later.
- Imagery: only the client's own Instagram photos and videos (1080px wide, vertical).
- Open: domain, final prices/durations, real booking backend.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Irina Dinga · Hairstylist · Valencia. Instagram @irinadistylist (verified), Threads, TikTok.
- Her own statement ("MI ENFOQUE", 2024): hair is an act of expressing identity, our calling card; it gives light, strength, sensuality, lightness, sophistication and enhances natural beauty.
- Instagram signature: shade names per work (Champagne Bronde, Almond Honey, Vanilla Blonde, Golden Brunette…), ✨ and 🤍.

## Product Principles

1. The work sells: show real results first, at full quality.
2. Honesty is the luxury: hair health over upselling is the core promise.
3. Booking must be one calm flow ending in WhatsApp, in the visitor's language.
4. Personal, not corporate: it is Irina's chair, not a chain.
